01 / THE BASICS

A tiny word with
a clear job.

Ask “to whom?” or “for whom?” after the verb. The answer is the indirect object.

person doing the action+indirect object pronoun+verb + thing

The six pronouns

The pronoun matches the receiver, not the thing being given.

meto / for me
nosto / for us
teto / for you (informal singular)
osto / for you all (informal, Spain)
leto / for him, her, you (formal singular)
lesto / for them, you all (formal / Latin America)
↳

Where does it go?

Usually before a conjugated verb: Le escribo. With an infinitive, you can also attach it: Voy a escribirle.

◎

Why say the name too?

Le can mean “to him,” “to her,” or formal “to you.” Add a Ana or a él to make the receiver clear: Le doy un libro a Ana.

✦

One special change

Before lo, la, los, or las, le and les become se: Se lo doy. (“I give it to her.”)
